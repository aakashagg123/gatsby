The post-training pipeline

Raw talent first, then shaping · each step is a small push

Later stages use far less data, so each one points what the model already has. A push can also move other things.

1Pretraining
web-scale text

Predict the next token. Builds language and facts.

2Supervised tuning
thousands of examples

Show the right answer. Teaches format, tone, instruction-following.

3Preference tuning
thousands of pairs

Better vs. worse answer. Teaches what people prefer. Can be gamed.

4Verifiable rewards
tasks with a check

Reward a passing test or a correct sum. Needs an automatic check.

Toy model, loss on ordinary text: pretrained 1.103 → after supervised tuning 2.280 → after preference tuning 2.677. Behaviour: P(answer) 0.083 → 0.987 → 1.000.

InstructGPT: about 13,000 supervised prompts, and under 2% of the pretraining compute for the largest tuning run.
