Why RAG

Bounded answers · closed book vs. open book

Retrieval doesn't just improve answers — it lets you tell the model to stop guessing.

User question"What's our EU refund window?"
Closed book · no RAG
Model guesses from training data
Guess plausibly
"30 days" · plausible, wrong, uncitable
Open book · RAG
Model reads from your passages
Retrieve passages
Assemble prompt + evidence
Answer from passages only
"14 days (Policy §4.2)" · grounded & citable
The extra sentence retrieval enables: "answer only from the passages; if they don't contain the answer, say so."
