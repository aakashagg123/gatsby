The four parts

Enforcement · decision · information · administration

Each part has an owner and a failure mode.

PEP · Enforcement pointIn the app or gateway. Asks, then obeys. Failure: missing on one endpoint.
PDP · Decision pointEvaluates the policy. Failure: slow, or fails open.
PIP · Information pointSupplies attributes and relationships. Failure: stale data.
PAP · Administration pointWhere policy is written. Failure: edited without review.
PEP asks PDP "may Priya edit report 7?" · PDP asks PIP for facts · PAP supplies the rules
