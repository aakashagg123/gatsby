Anatomy of a .bpmn20.xml file

Three nested pieces — only one of them the engine reads

<BPMNDiagram> is optional; the engine ignores it.

<definitions> — namespaces, targetNamespace
↓
<process> — the executable graph (this is what the engine reads)
↓
<BPMNDiagram> — x/y coordinates for rendering (optional; the engine ignores it)
