Ladder of costs

Each rung is roughly a thousand times slower

Keep the hot path on the upper rungs.

1Memory
about 100 ns

A cache or in-process read.

2SSD read
16 to 100 µs

Random read, order of magnitude.

3Data center round trip
about 0.5 ms

One network hop.

4Disk seek
about 10 ms

A spinning disk.

5Ocean crossing
about 150 ms

California to Europe.

Exact values vary by hardware. The ratios are what matter.
