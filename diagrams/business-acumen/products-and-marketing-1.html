Four go-to-market motions

Per merchant, payments company. Illustrative; all inputs assumed. Contribution is per month, after any revenue share and the cost to serve.

Self-serve

Customer signs up alone. Small online merchant.

Revenue share0%

Contribution$60

CAC$400

Payback6.7 mo

Fits: simple product, small deals. Breaks: on churn.

Sales-led

A sales team closes a contract. Mid-sized merchant.

Revenue share0%

Contribution$600

CAC$10,071

Payback16.8 mo

Fits: large deals. Breaks: below about $933 a month of net revenue.

Partner-led

A partner refers or resells. Mid-sized merchant.

Revenue share25%

Contribution$350

CAC$4,250

Payback12.1 mo

Fits: partner holds the trust. Breaks: a share for life costs more than it saves.

Embedded

Sold inside the merchant's software. Small merchant.

Revenue share30%

Contribution$53.33

CAC$400

Payback7.5 mo

Fits: buying happens in the software. Breaks: if the partner switches provider.

Lower acquisition cost usually means a larger revenue share. Compare motions on lifetime contribution after the share, and on payback.
