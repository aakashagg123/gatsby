Seven sources of advantage

Each needs a benefit (lower cost or higher price) and a barrier (why rivals cannot copy it).

Cost advantagesyou deliver the same thing for less

Scale economies

Barrier: a rival needs your volume

Test: cost per unit falls with volume

Erodes: rivals rent scale as a service

Process power

Barrier: routines built over years

Test: rivals tried for 2+ years and fell short

Erodes: vendors package the process

Demand advantagescustomers value you more or stay longer

Network effects

Barrier: a rival needs your network

Test: retention rises with density

Erodes: cheap multi-homing

Switching costs

Barrier: the customer's cost to move

Test: cost to switch > rival's saving

Erodes: portability rules, standard APIs

Brand

Barrier: years of trust

Test: better conversion at equal price

Erodes: a breach or a scandal

Structural advantagesaccess or a model rivals cannot or will not copy

Cornered resource

Barrier: exclusive access

Test: would the partner sign your rival?

Erodes: licences open up, contracts end

Counter-positioning

Barrier: copying harms the incumbent

Test: the incumbent's best response loses it money

Erodes: the old business shrinks

A benefit with no barrier is a head start, not an advantage.
