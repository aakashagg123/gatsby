Cohort payback

Cumulative CM2 per acquired merchant, by month

Illustrative (worked example B). CM2 $250 a month. Churn 8% a month for three months, then 2%.

dashed: simple payback, no churnsolid: cohort payback, with churn

Simple payback divides CAC by one survivor's margin. Cohort payback divides it by the average acquired merchant's margin, and that average shrinks as merchants leave.
