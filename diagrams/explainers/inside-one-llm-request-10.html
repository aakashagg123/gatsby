The whole journey

One LLM request, step by step

From pressing Enter to the last word on your screen.

The request arrives

A gateway checks who you are and your limits. Then the request waits for a free machine.

The question becomes 6 tokens (an illustrative split) and shares the context window.

  • tokens

3
Read it all at once

Prefill reads every prompt token in one parallel pass.

  • parallel pass
  • KV cache is born
  • wait for the first word
4
Write one token at a time

Decode adds one token per step, reading the KV cache, and picks each word from probabilities.

  • one token per step
  • cache grows
  • sampling
5
The answer streams back

Tokens go to your screen as they are made. One machine serves many people at once.

  • your screen
  • batching
