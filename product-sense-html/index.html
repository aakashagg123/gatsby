Product sense for the AI PM

The instinct for what makes a product succeed — sharpened for the AI era.

Core insight

Product sense is six habits that compound, and the AI capstone puts them to work. For AI, the product is the trustworthy system around a model that can be confidently wrong.

7 lessons+ recapfor APMs & PMsAI-native

Product sense is the instinct for what makes a product succeed. It shows up as the ability to see what users need, including the needs they never say out loud, and to shape a solution that feels right to them. People talk about it as a gift, but it behaves much more like a skill, one you sharpen through repeated contact with users and deliberate practice. For a PM moving into AI product management, it decides whether you lead the conversation about an AI feature or just nod along in it. A model is a new and probabilistic material, and product sense is what tells you what to build with it, for whom, and why.

This module has seven lessons. Six are habits that compound into strong product sense, and the seventh, the AI capstone, applies all of them to building products on top of a model.

A trustworthy system around the model
  1. Motivation theory covers the behaviour model, friction, satisfaction and ethical nudges, so you can see why a motivated user still doesn't act.
  2. Cognitive empathy covers thinking about your own thinking, changing your mind, simulating the user and building taste.
  3. User research covers matching the method to the question, interviewing without leading, and researching trust in AI products.
  4. Creativity runs from strategy (positioning, focus) to execution (rituals, techniques and AI prototyping).
  5. Communication covers artifacts, reviewing AI-drafted documents, earning buy-in, and product-sense interviews.
  6. Domain expertise is about turning industry knowledge into intuition, building it fast, and not getting trapped by it.
  7. Product sense for AI products is the capstone. It covers what changes when the material is a probabilistic model, from evals as taste and trust design to scoping and keeping a person in the loop.

Each lesson opens with a situation, works through one example step by step, and ends with things to do next. Several include short, credited moments from practitioners who have been guests on The Growth Podcast with Aakash Gupta (news.aakashg.com).
